How it works

Three steps, no ongoing subscription.

The bureau exists to do one job well: connect a salon that needs a chair filled with a professional ready to fill it, and confirm the match properly.

01

Post the open chair

Salon owner lists the role, location, pay structure and start date. Free to post — no fee until a hire is confirmed.

Salon
02

Review matched candidates

The bureau shortlists vetted professionals for the role. Salon reviews profiles and picks who to interview.

Bureau
03

Confirm the hire, pay the fee

Once the salon confirms, pay the flat placement fee via M-Pesa. Contact details are released and you take it from there.

Both parties
